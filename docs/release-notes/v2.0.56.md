# Version 2.0.56

## CHZZK live continuity

- Adapt Chzzk-Rekoda's LL-HLS continuity improvements to Phrolova's Streamlink capture pipeline.
- Prefer the domestic CDN and prepare an Akamai standby path only after validating playback segments and initialization data.
- Receive published partial segments and recover missing/incomplete intervals with full segments, with bounded recovery and shutdown handling.
- Split files and jobs when initialization data changes, preserving the active recording's quality and storage policy.
- Keep Streamlink capture followed by FFmpeg finalization and FFprobe inspection. Prefer TS and preserve actual fMP4 source format when needed.
- Show receiving CDN/standby status in recording details without exposing credentials or signed URLs in diagnostic events.

## Card alignment

- Keep adjacent collapsed live cards compact when a preview expands.
- Remove excess space in download grids and align card heights and bottom actions, including quality/start controls on queued cards.
